## Introduction  

<p align="justify">
- VLAs unify vision, language and action data at scale, to learn policies that generalize across diverse tasks, different robotic embodiements and environments
- the goal is to enable more flexible and scalable real-world deployments through the advanced generalization capabilities, with no or significantly less task-specific training data
- definition: a system that takes visual observations and natural language instructions as required inputs (+ additional data like proprioceptive data or point clouds or whatever), it outputs robot actions by directly generating control commands
</p>
